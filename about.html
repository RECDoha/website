About REC

One student in a dining room, to Qatar's top ten.

Licensed by the Ministry of Education since 2013, REC teaches both Pearson Edexcel and Cambridge International (CIE) syllabi under the umbrella of the British Curriculum — English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and Biology, from Year 1 to Year 13.

Top 10 in Qatar · 2019

The story so far

2003
It begins in Ms Rifka's dining room, with a single student.
2013
REC is established as a centre, licensed by the Ministry of Education & Higher Education (CR 63429).
2019
Named among Qatar's Top-10 education centres — the only British Curriculum support centre awarded.
Today
116 students on roll, and the same homely, relaxed atmosphere it started with.

How lessons run

Lessons are continuous and regular through the year — students attend every lesson and arrive on time.

01
1–2 hour sessions
Length depends on the subject and year group.
02
Full syllabus, every year
The entire syllabus for each year group is completed within the academic year.
03
Worksheets & homework
Set adequately after every lesson to consolidate what was covered.
04
Regular testing
Customised tests monitor each student's progress against the standard syllabus.
English · Yr 1–11 Maths · Yr 1–13 Physics · Yr 7–13 Chemistry · Yr 7–13 Biology · Yr 7–11 Edexcel & Cambridge (CIE)
